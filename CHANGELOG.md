# Changelog

All notable changes to Abduct, one version per feature.

## 0.7.1 — Hold on

- Holding a poster, link or text on your phone no longer opens the browser's menu (Open in new tab, Save image) or selects the text. Holding in a text field still lets you paste.

## 0.7.0 — Where to watch

- A title's page shows where you can watch it in your country: streaming, free, to rent or to buy. For anime, the services that stream it.
- Discover shows what's popular in your country, and every row ends in See all: keep scrolling through trending, popular or any genre.
- Browse by genre, for movies, series and anime.
- Put a title on any of your lists from its page: the button beside Add opens all of them.
- Abduct works out your country from your time zone, or pick it yourself in Settings.

## 0.6.0 — Along for the ride

- A little UFO keeps you company on every page. Leave Home and it shrinks out of the big one and lands on the new page. It perches on the corner of whatever card you tap, hover over or hold your finger on, and follows along as you scroll.
- 30 new list icons: zombies, vampires, aliens, robots, comics, anime, swords, guns, slasher, pirates, ninjas, spies, knights, spiders, apocalypse, time travel, magic, rockets, explosive, racing, games, books, series, classics, awards, trending, kids, animals, winter and ocean.
- Making or editing a list is quicker: the icons show three rows until you open them all, and Save stays at the bottom of the screen.

## 0.5.0 — Install it

- Put Abduct on your home screen: a bar at the top offers to install it (on iPhone: Share, then Add to Home Screen), and it opens in its own window like an app.
- It opens faster once installed, and shows a "Lost the signal" page instead of an error when you're offline.
- Settings shows which version you're on; tap it for What's new, every change so far.
- Tap any search result or trending poster to see its details: story, genres, what people say, and Add.
- Added something by mistake? Undo from the bar that pops up at the bottom.

## 0.4.2 — Liftoff

- The UFO flies between Home and Pick for me: tap Pick for me and it lifts off the card and banks into place above the beam, then flies back when you go Home. Its beam switches on once it lands.

## 0.4.1 — Oops, undo

- Added something by mistake? Tap its check to take it back off, or tap Undo under it. Works in search, Discover and on a title's page.
- Your lists on Home are rows now: every list shows, with its full name, color and newest posters.
- A star in a list's top bar makes it your default list in one tap (it's filled on the one that is).

## 0.4.0 — Share and discover

- Discover: what's new and trending in movies, series and anime (tap + to add one), what people on Abduct rate highest, and lists people share.
- Make a list public from its edit page and anyone on Abduct can find it in Discover, see what's on it, like it and add from it. What you've watched stays yours.
- Comments can be for everyone now: others see them on the title, with your name, and you see theirs under "What others said". Switch a comment to "Only you" before posting to keep it to yourself. Comments from before this update stay private.
- Choose your name on Abduct in Settings.
- Make any list your default: it's where titles go when you add without choosing.
- Arrange your lists, and the titles on a list, in your own order: tap Arrange and drag.
- Home has more room: no top bar (Settings is in the corner of the card), and Pick for me and Add sit side by side.

## 0.3.0 — Sort it later

- In a hurry? Add a title without choosing a list: it goes to your default list (your Watchlist), and you can sort it later.
- A title's page shows which of your lists it's on. Tap a list to put it there too, or tap again to take it off.
- Your default list is marked as such. You can rename it and change its look, but not delete it, so there's always somewhere for new titles to land.

## 0.2.0 — Stars and colors

- Rate what you've watched, from one star (Not for me) to five (Loved it). Tap the same star again to take it off. Your stars show under each title in a list.
- Say what you thought: every title has its own thread of comments, dated, so you can add to it over time.
- After the UFO's pick, mark it watched and rate it right there.
- Give each list an icon and a color, so you can tell them apart at a glance. The icon takes the list's color everywhere it shows.
- Choosing which list to add to, and a genre in Pick for me, now open a proper menu instead of the phone's plain one.
- The UFO flies level, with its beam coming straight down from underneath.

## 0.1.0 — First contact

- Keep lists of movies, series and anime you want to watch. Everyone starts with a Watchlist, and you can make more.
- Search movies, series and anime and add them with one tap, or add anything by hand.
- Can't decide? *Pick for me* lets the UFO choose. Narrow it down by list, kind, how much time you have, genre, or rewatches. Titles that have waited longest get a better chance, and ones it offered lately come up less.
- Said yes to a pick? It shows on Home as tonight's pick.
- Mark titles watched from the title or straight from the pick.
- Sign in with Google, or try it as a guest.
- Install it to your phone's home screen.
